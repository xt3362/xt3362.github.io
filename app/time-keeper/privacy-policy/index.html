Privacy Policy

Introduction

TimeKeeper is a timer application designed to help you manage your time effectively. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

TimeKeeper does not collect any personal information. You are not required to create an account, provide your name, email address, or any other personal data to use this application.

Locally Stored Data

TimeKeeper stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Timer settings (duration, name, display preferences)
  • App settings (theme mode, display options)

This data is stored solely on your device and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

Third-Party Services

TimeKeeper uses Google Fonts to download and display custom fonts. When fonts are loaded, your device may connect to Google's servers to retrieve font files. Google's privacy policy applies to this data transfer. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data with third parties. Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to share.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall TimeKeeper, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted. You can also clear the app data through your device settings at any time.

Children's Privacy

TimeKeeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any personal information from any user, it is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
